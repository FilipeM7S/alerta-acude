# COGERH trophic-state (IET) reports

Public reports by COGERH (Companhia de Gestão dos Recursos Hídricos do Ceará), one per quarterly campaign.
Listed on the Portal Hidrológico do Ceará, Qualidade > Eutrofização > Relatório: https://www.hidro.ce.gov.br/documentos/152
(the old address portalhidrologico.ce.gov.br no longer resolves). Files are served by the Funceme CDN.
That list has 28 reports; all 28 are here. The site has no robots.txt; files were fetched one at a time with a pause.
Files were renamed `iet_YYYY_MM.pdf` after the campaign printed in each report's Table 3 header.
